FAQ

Common Questions

How much does a website cost?

Websites start from ₹1,500 for a single-page portfolio or landing page, ₹6,000 for a full business website, and ₹35,000 for a complete e-commerce store. Final pricing depends on pages, features and requirements — tell us what you need and we'll give you an exact quote.

How long does it take to build?

Simple portfolios can be delivered in 1–4 days, business websites typically take a bit longer, and e-commerce stores usually take 10–14 days. Exact delivery time depends on the package and scope you choose.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Every website we build is fully responsive and tested across mobile, tablet and desktop screens.

Do you provide domain and hosting?

Some packages, like our E-Commerce Pro plan, include free domain and hosting for the first year. For other packages, we can guide you through setup or help you connect your own domain and hosting.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes, we redesign existing websites to make them faster, more modern and more conversion-focused. Select "Website Redesign" in the form above and share your current site link.

Can I request changes?

Yes. Every package includes a set number of revisions so you can fine-tune the design before launch — the exact number depends on the plan you choose.

How do we communicate during the project?

Mainly over WhatsApp and email, so you always know what stage your project is at and can share feedback quickly.
