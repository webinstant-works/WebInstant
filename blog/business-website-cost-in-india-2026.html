How Much Does a Business Website Cost in India in 2026?

Website prices in India range from a few thousand rupees to several lakhs. Here is what drives the cost and how to choose a budget that fits your business.

"How much will a website cost me?" is usually the first question an Indian business owner asks, and the answers online are confusing. One person says ₹3,000, another says ₹3 lakh. Both can be right, because "a website" can mean anything from a one-page profile to a custom online store with payment gateways.

This guide breaks down what actually influences the price, the typical ranges in 2026, the recurring costs people forget, and how to decide on a sensible budget. Prices below are indicative market ranges and vary by agency, features and scope; they usually exclude GST.

What decides the cost of a website?

  • Type of website: a portfolio, a business site and an online store need very different amounts of work.
  • Number of pages: every extra page needs design, content layout and testing.
  • Design level: a clean template-based design costs less than fully custom UI and animation.
  • Features: contact forms, booking, payment gateways, user accounts, multilingual content and integrations add time.
  • SEO and speed work: proper on-page SEO, performance tuning and sitemaps are part of a quality build.
  • Content: if you need copywriting, photography or logo design, expect additional cost.
  • Who builds it: a freelancer, a small agency and a large firm price very differently.

Domain name and hosting: the recurring basics

Domain name

Your web address, such as yourbusiness.in or yourbusiness.com, is typically renewed annually. Common extensions usually cost roughly ₹500 to ₹1,500 per year, while premium or short names can cost much more.

Web hosting

Hosting is where your site's files live. Shared hosting for a small site generally runs a few thousand rupees a year, while dedicated or cloud hosting for busy stores costs more. Many hosts include a free SSL certificate; if yours does not, secure HTTPS is still essential. Our hosting and domain setup service can help you choose and configure both.

Maintenance and updates: the cost people forget

A website is not a poster you print once. Software needs updates, backups should run regularly, content changes as your services change, and security must be monitored. Freelancers and agencies commonly charge anywhere from about ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 per month for small business sites, and more for online stores.

Hidden costs to ask about

  • Extra charges for additional revisions or pages
  • Paid themes, plugins or premium fonts
  • Payment gateway setup and transaction fees for online stores
  • Content writing, product photography or logo design
  • Annual renewal of domain and hosting after the first year
  • GST on the final invoice

Cheap, mid-range or premium: how to choose your budget

Start with your goal, not the price

A dentist who wants appointment enquiries needs a trustworthy, fast, mobile-friendly site with clear services and a contact form. They do not need a complex web app. A fashion label that wants to sell online needs a catalogue, cart and payments. The goal sets the scope, and the scope sets the price.

Compare value, not just numbers

A very low quote can look attractive, but check what is missing: mobile optimisation, SEO setup, speed tuning, revisions, support and ownership of your files. A slightly higher price that includes these is often the cheaper option over two years.

Use this simple rule of thumb

  • Just need an online presence? A portfolio or starter business site is enough.
  • Want leads from Google? Choose a multi-page business site with proper SEO.
  • Want to sell products? Budget for an e-commerce build plus ongoing maintenance.

Is a website worth the investment?

For most businesses, a single extra customer a month can pay for a starter site within a year, and the website keeps working long after. Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum cost to build a website in India?

A simple portfolio or one-page site can start from a few thousand rupees, while a professional multi-page business website commonly starts around ₹6,000 and goes up depending on pages and features.

Do I have to pay for the website every year?

You pay the build cost once, but the domain and hosting renew annually. Maintenance is optional but recommended to keep the site secure and up to date.

Why do quotes from different agencies vary so much?

Scope, design quality, SEO work, support, experience and included extras all differ. Always compare what is included, not just the headline price.

Is GST charged on website development?

GST is generally applicable on professional services and is usually added to the final invoice if the agency is registered. Confirm this in the quote before you sign.

Can I start small and upgrade the website later?

Yes. A well-built site can grow by adding pages, a blog, booking or an online store as your business expands.
