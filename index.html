FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Is domain and hosting included?

Domain and hosting are not included by default, but can be arranged separately as an add-on. We'll guide you through the entire setup so you don't have to figure it out alone.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes, every website we build is fully responsive and tested across mobile, tablet, and desktop. Your visitors will get a great experience on any screen size.

Can I request changes after delivery?

Absolutely. Minor revisions are included with every plan. The number of free revisions depends on your package, and we're always flexible to ensure you're happy with the result.

How will we communicate during the project?

All discussions, updates, and deliveries happen through WhatsApp and email — fast, convenient, and everything in one place so nothing gets missed.

How much does a professional website cost in India?

Our plans start at ₹1,999 for a simple landing page, with most small business websites ranging between ₹6,000–₹10,000. E-commerce and custom builds are priced based on scope. Check our Plans page for full pricing details.

Is WebInstant a good web design agency for small businesses in Delhi?

Yes — WebInstant is a Delhi-based web design agency that specializes in affordable, fast-turnaround websites for small businesses, clinics, gyms, startups, and freelancers across Delhi NCR and the rest of India.
