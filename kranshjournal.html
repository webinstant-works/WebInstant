01 Project Objective

The goal is to create a professional digital home for The Kransh Journal where visitors can understand the brand, explore health education content, connect with the newsletter, and learn more about Dr. Archana and her work.

Since The Kransh Journal is currently in its early stage, this Phase 1 starter website will focus on creating a strong and professional foundation without adding unnecessary complexity or cost.

The website will also be structured with future growth in mind, allowing additional features to be added as the brand develops. The idea is simple: build the professional foundation now, then expand the website as The Kransh Journal grows.

02 Website Features

The core pages that will make up the website:

Home

Introduction to The Kransh Journal, its purpose, and the value it provides to its audience.

About

Information about Dr. Archana, her background, the vision behind the journal, and the purpose of the brand.

The Journal

A dedicated section for presenting health education articles and directing visitors toward published content.

Note: The Phase 1 website does not include a CMS or admin panel for uploading or editing articles. Initial content will be added during development based on the material provided by the client.

Featured Content

A section to highlight selected, important, or recent articles and resources.

Newsletter

Integration with the existing Substack newsletter so visitors can discover and subscribe to The Kransh Journal.

Videos

A dedicated section prepared for future short-form and long-form video content, including YouTube integration when the channel is launched.

Contact

Relevant contact information and social/contact links for visitors who wish to connect.

This is a focused Phase 1 starter scope built to establish a professional brand presence. It does not include a CMS, admin panel, backend, database, or automated content management — these can be added in later phases as the brand grows.

04 Future-Ready Structure

The first version will focus on establishing the brand and creating a professional online presence.

Start Simple Grow Expand

As The Kransh Journal grows, the website can later be expanded with:

Health guides & digital products Online payment functionality Premium health magazine Content Management System (CMS) Admin dashboard for publishing Membership / subscription features Advanced analytics Additional video features Other digital products & resources
These features are not included in the initial website development and can be developed separately when they become necessary — allowing the brand to grow gradually instead of investing in complex systems before they are actually needed.

06 Domain & Hosting

Domain

A custom domain such as thekranshjournal.com or thekranshjournal.in can be purchased separately by the client. For long-term ownership and easier management, the domain should ideally be registered under the client's own account. The cost of the domain and its future renewal is separate from the website development fee.

Hosting

For the initial static website, GitHub Pages can be used as the hosting platform at no additional hosting cost. If the website later requires a backend, CMS, database, payment system, membership system, or other server-side functionality, the hosting infrastructure can be upgraded accordingly. Any future hosting or third-party service costs will be discussed separately before implementation.
