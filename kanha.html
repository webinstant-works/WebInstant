01 — About

Who's building this

I'm Kanha, a web developer who likes turning plain ideas into fast, tidy websites. Most of my work happens through WebInstant, the studio I founded to help small businesses, creators, and startups get online without the usual delays — good design, working code, shipped quickly.

My day-to-day covers the full front end and increasingly the back end too: structuring pages that make sense on a phone first, writing CSS that doesn't fight itself, and adding just enough JavaScript to make things feel alive. I care about pages that load quickly, read clearly, and hold up on every screen size — not just the one on my desk.

Outside client work, I keep building — productivity trackers, small e-commerce concepts, learning tools for other beginner developers — because shipping real projects is still the fastest way I know to get better.
