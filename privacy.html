1 Information We Collect

A. Personal Information (Provided by You)

When you contact or purchase our services, we may collect:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Business / Project Details
  • Billing Information

B. Automatically Collected Data

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP Address
  • Browser Type & Device Information
  • Pages Visited
  • Time Spent on Website
  • Referral Links
  • Cookies & Usage Data

C. Payment Information

Payments are processed via secure third-party gateways (Razorpay, PayPal, Stripe, etc.)

We do NOT store your full payment details. All transactions are securely handled by PCI-compliant providers.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use your data only to run and improve our services:

  • Service Delivery — provide and manage your project
  • Communication — communicate about projects
  • Payments — process payments securely
  • Improvement — improve website experience

We NEVER sell, rent, or misuse your personal data.

3 Information Sharing

We only share data with trusted parties when absolutely necessary:

Trusted Service Providers

  • Payment processors (Razorpay, Stripe, PayPal)
  • Hosting providers
  • Email/communication services
  • Analytics tools (Google Analytics)

Legal Requirements

We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of users.

4 Cookies Policy

We use cookies to improve your experience on our website:

  • Essential Cookies — required for the website to function properly. Cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies — help us understand how visitors interact with our website.
  • Preference Cookies — remember your settings and preferences for future visits.

You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings, though some features may not work correctly.

5 Data Security

We employ strong security measures to protect your data:

  • Secure servers
  • Controlled access
  • Regular monitoring

Disclaimer: No system is 100% secure. While we do everything possible to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6 Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access Your Data — request a copy of the data we hold
  • Update Information — correct inaccurate or outdated data
  • Request Deletion — ask us to delete your personal data
  • Opt-Out — unsubscribe from marketing emails
  • Data Portability — request a portable copy of your data

To exercise any of these rights, email us at businesswebinstant@gmail.com

7 Data Retention

We retain your data:

  • For as long as needed to deliver and support our services

Once no longer needed, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

8 Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites or services. These are provided for your convenience.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their policies independently.

9 Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If we discover that data from a child under 13 has been collected, it will be deleted immediately upon request.
